📖 to do or doing?

После глаголовЦель · too/enough · it + adjПредлоги и фразыМеняется смысл

1. После определённых глаголов

+ to do (infinitive)

afford ask agree appear arrange attempt be able choose decide expect fail hope happen intend learn manage offer plan prepare pretend promise refuse seem want wish would like would love would prefer demand

He promised to be back at 10.

They decided to go there together.

+ doing (-ing form)

admit appreciate avoid can't help consider continue deny can't stand delay dislike enjoy feel like finish imagine involve mind miss practise suggest like love prefer hate detest start stop fancy

She enjoys playing the guitar.

She likes playing the guitar.

Continue doing it like this, and you will see.

⚠️ like / love / prefer + doing, но would like / would love / would prefer + to do: I like travelling. → I'd like to travel to Japan.

2. Ещё правила

to do — чтобы выразить цель (зачем?)

We need to optimise the supply chain to reduce costs.

doing — как подлежащее

Smoking is dangerous for your health.

Implementing new technologies involves integrating systems and training staff.

to do — после too / enough

too + adjective (+ for someone) + to do

adjective + enough + to do

enough + noun (+ for someone) + to do

The budget is enough for us to expand our distribution network.

The team is skilled enough to manage large-scale operations effectively.

We have enough resources to complete the project on time.

The team is too busy to take on another project.

to do — после It / noun + be + adjective

Когда говорим, что сделать важно, легко, нужно, правильно…

It is important to review the logistics plan thoroughly.

It is easy to overlook small errors in large shipments.

3. После предлогов, фразовых глаголов и устойчивых фраз → всегда doing

Предлоги и фразовые глаголы

be good/bad at be capable of be interested in look forward to talk about think of succeed in accuse of blame for be afraid of complain of prevent smb from persist in be engaged in suspect of give up be fond of be proud of insist on depend on object to put off be surprised at be used to What about…? How about…?

I am good at playing the piano. I am good at drawing.

I am afraid of going abroad.

I'm looking forward to seeing you! (to здесь предлог!)

This training is essential for managing inventory effectively.

She's interested in expanding the distribution network.

This lesson is important for knowing how to communicate with clients.

Устойчивые фразы

there's no point (in) waste time can't help it's no use be worth have difficulty have trouble spend time be/get used to

I spend my whole evening reading about HTML and CSS.

It's worth considering implementing a new strategy.

The team has difficulty managing unexpected supply chain disruptions.

There's no point (in) waiting. Let's go.

4. Можно и так и так, но смысл меняется

+ to do+ doing
forget
забыть сделать (это не сделано)

I forgot to send the report.

I didn't remember to send it, so it's still not done.
забыть то, что было

I will never forget meeting her for the first time.

I will always remember that moment.
remember
не забыть сделать (будущее)

Remember to call the supplier tomorrow.

Make sure you call: it's important.
помнить, как было (прошлое)

I remember visiting that factory last year.

I have a memory of the visit.
stop
остановиться, чтобы… (цель)

She stopped to check her email.

She paused what she was doing to check her email.
перестать, бросить

He stopped smoking last year.

He doesn't smoke any more.
try
намагатися / пытаться (усилие)

We'll try to resolve the issue by the end of the day.

We'll make an effort.
пробувати / попробовать как вариант

Try restarting the system to fix the problem.

Experiment: maybe it will work.
regret
с сожалением сообщить (официально)

We regret to inform you that your application was unsuccessful.

We are sorry to tell you.
жалеть о том, что сделал(а)

I regret not attending the conference last year.

I'm sorry I didn't go.
like
привычка, «считаю правильным»

I like to take breaks during work.

I prefer to take breaks regularly.
нравится сам процесс

I like taking breaks during work.

I enjoy taking breaks.
hate
очень не хочется (но надо)

I hate to ask for help, but I need it.

I don't like asking, but it's necessary.
ненавижу (вообще)

I hate waiting in long lines.

I strongly dislike waiting.
💡 doing смотрит назад (это уже было), to do смотрит вперёд (это нужно сделать).
